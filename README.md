# Dad Joke Showdown — Bank Interest

An entry for the [Dad Joke Showdown](https://make.fun/c/u-mfnakmal-dad-joke-showdown-1d2ce3e5) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun): one dad joke, at most 200 characters, written to be told out loud at the dinner table.

## The joke

> I took my son to the bank to open an account. He asked what interest he'd get. I said, 'With your grades, son — none.'

## Why this joke

The pun turns on one word doing two jobs: *interest* as the money a bank pays you, and *interest* as caring about school. The setup is a father and son at the bank, the turn is understood on the first read, and the punchline lands on the last word — a dad delivering the verdict across the table.

The joke is 118 characters, inside the 200-character gate.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt`. It runs on Node 22.18+ with no dependencies, no network access, and no environment secrets — everything the entry runs is in this repository. A built-in guard refuses to write an entry outside the 1–200 character gate, so the sealed scorer always sees a valid file.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
